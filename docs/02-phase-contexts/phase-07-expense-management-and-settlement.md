# Phase 07 — Expense Management and Settlement

This is an independently designed business module within the Trip Planner. It helps groups record shared expenses, calculate individual balances, and settle payments fairly.

Investigate individually:
- Creating, editing, and deleting expenses.
- Expense categories.
- Multiple payers.
- Equal splitting.
- Custom amount splitting.
- Percentage-based splitting.
- Member-specific shares.
- Including and excluding members.
- Individual and net balances.
- Simplifying settlements.
- Recording settlements.
- Settlement history.
- Reports and exports.
- Expense corrections.

Understand the mathematics and business rules before implementation. Focus on decimal precision, rounding, transaction consistency, financial history, balance calculation, and settlement correctness. Keep it a well-isolated module inside the modular monolith, not a separate MVP microservice.
