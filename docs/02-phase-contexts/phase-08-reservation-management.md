# Phase 08 — Reservation Management

The objective is to give trip members one place to organize booking and reservation information.

Investigate individually:
- Hotel, flight, train, and bus reservations.
- Restaurant reservations.
- Activity and attraction bookings.
- Reservation details.
- Booking references and confirmation numbers.
- Dates, times, amounts, and payment details.
- Booking provider or website.
- Attachments and confirmation documents.
- Notes.
- Reservation status.
- Editing and cancellation tracking.

The MVP primarily supports manually entered information. Understand how different reservation types can share a common domain model while retaining type-specific attributes. Explore storage, validation, lifecycle management, and future integrations.
