# Phase 09 — Journal, Media, and Completed Trips

The objective is to help users preserve trip experiences during and after the journey.

Investigate individually:
- Completing a trip.
- Viewing completed trips.
- Journal entries.
- Day-wise journal organization.
- Notes and descriptions.
- Trip photo uploads.
- Media attached to places and activities.
- Member contributions.
- Journal entries associated with visited locations.
- Editing completed trip information.
- Preserving historical records.
- Deleting a completed trip.
- Access to historical content.

Understand how journaling and trip execution data connect without unnecessary dependencies. Focus on media storage, metadata, access control, historical consistency, and the difference between editing a journal and modifying trip history.
