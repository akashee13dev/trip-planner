# Phase 02 — Membership, Invitations, Permissions, and Collaboration

The product's primary differentiation is group travel collaboration. Multiple users participate in a trip with different permissions and responsibilities.

Investigate individually:
- Email invitations.
- Accepting and rejecting invitations.
- Pending, expired, cancelled, and duplicate invitations.
- Trip membership.
- Trip-scoped Owner, Admin, Editor, and Viewer roles.
- Role changes and member removal.
- Member leaving a trip.
- Ownership transfer.
- Comments, reactions, and discussions.
- Polls and group decisions.
- Approval of suggestions before itinerary updates.
- Activity history and audit trails.

Focus on permission models, invitation lifecycle, authorization, collaboration workflows, concurrent updates, and preserving member contributions.
