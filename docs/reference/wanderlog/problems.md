What problem is the customer experiencing?

    - Places are scattered across Google Maps, Instagram and YouTube    :  Centralized place discovery
    - Friends discuss destinations in WhatsApp	: Collaborative trip planning
    - Nobody knows which place to visit on which day   :	Day-wise itinerary
    - Travel distances aren't considered	: Location-aware itinerary with real Map Integration
    - One person pays for everything    :	Group expense management
    - Booking details are scattered across emails	: Reservation management
    - We forget what happened during the trip	Trip journal
    - Who will use this for travel : Solo / Group / Family / Couple
    
Everyone: a universal travel planner.

But there's an important product strategy challenge here.

A universal product doesn't mean every customer needs every feature. It means we build a common travel-planning foundation that accommodates different travel styles.

For example:
Customer	Most important needs
Solo traveler	Discovery, itinerary, personal budget
Friends	Collaboration, expense splitting, voting
Family	Accommodation, accessibility, family-friendly places
Couples	Experiences, itinerary, shared memories
